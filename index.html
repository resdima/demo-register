The joint monthly status note, drafted from the register

Data rule: every figure carries its date, unit, indicator and source. The note is internal by default; a public summary appears only with both sides’ consent (Art. 10). It is issued by the 5th working day of each month and builds on Uzbekistan’s weekly progress reports.

Pilot rules and targets

What the pilot tests in 90 days

Rules

  • Every issue enters the register with a source, a proposed owner and a deadline in working days from registration.
  • The clock moves an open issue up one level after 7, 14 and 30 working days. These are pilot parameters, reviewed after three months.
  • An issue closes only with evidence.
  • The register and the note stay between the parties (Art. 10).
  • Supply volumes and tariffs stay in commercial contracts (Art. 9).
  • Financing and the ADB grant go to a separate track, where partners decide.

Targets during the pilot (to be tested)

  • 100% of registered issues have an owner and a deadline.
  • 80% of issues have a documented decision within 30 working days. This is a pilot objective, not a guaranteed result.
  • 3 of 3 monthly notes are issued by the 5th working day.
  • Median working days from registration to decision are measured monthly and should fall.
